# SOP-CDU-04: Crude Distillation Unit Piping Maintenance & Pressure Derating

**Document ID**: SOP-REF-CDU-04-2026  
**Category**: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)  
**Standard Authority**: ASME B31.3 / API 570 / PSU Reliability Directive  
**Effective Date**: 01 January 2026  
**Security Classification**: Sovereign PSU Proprietary — On-Premises Only  

---

## 1. Scope and Operational Boundaries
This procedure applies to all high-temperature atmospheric and vacuum distillation transfer lines within CDU-04, specifically including Line 14-P-102 (Crude Pre-flash Tower Bottoms to Vacuum Furnace).

- **Nominal Pipe Size (NPS)**: 14-inch Schedule 40 (Carbon Steel ASTM A106 Gr. B)
- **Nominal Wall Thickness**: 12.7 mm
- **Normal Operating Pressure**: 18.5 bar
- **Design Operating Temperature**: 340°C (Maximum allowable: 360°C)
- **Fluid Service**: Heavy Sour Crude / Reduced Crude Residue

---

## 2. Inspection Protocols & Thickness Gauging
1. Ultrasonic thickness testing (UTT) must be conducted at designated TML (Thickness Measurement Locations) at intervals not exceeding 60 days for active naphthenic acid corrosion loops.
2. High-wear locations, specifically 90-degree elbows (e.g. Elbow EL-04) and reducer junctions, require grid scanning (minimum 16 grid points).
3. Minimum structural retirement thickness ($t_{min}$) for Line 14-P-102 under ASME B31.3 formula is calculated as **5.4 mm**.

---

## 3. Mandatory Pressure Derating Thresholds (Section 4.1 & 4.2)
### Section 4.1: Immediate Derating Trigger
When ultrasonic measurement at any point on Line 14-P-102 reveals a wall thickness below **8.0 mm** (representing greater than 37% wall loss against nominal 12.7 mm):
- The Operations Shift Superintendent must immediately issue an engineering derating directive.
- Operating pressure must be derated from **18.5 bar to a maximum operating limit of 12.0 bar** within 4 hours of measurement confirmation.
- Direct crude bypass valves must be tested and verified for emergency depressurization capability.

### Section 4.2: Operating Temperature Controls
- During derated operation at 12.0 bar, operating temperature must not exceed **340°C**.
- Continuous thermocouple telemetry from sensors TT-102A and TT-102B must be monitored in the DCS control room.

---

## 4. Temporary Clamp Enclosure Protocols (Section 5.1 & 5.2)
### Section 5.1: Clamp Box Specification
Installation of a high-temperature engineered clamp enclosure on Elbow EL-04 is authorized under the following conditions:
- Enclosure material must match or exceed ASTM A516 Gr. 70 with high-temperature graphite sealant packing.
- Maximum allowable interim service duration is **90 days**, during which permanent spool replacement must be procured and staged.

### Section 5.2: Containment Design Criteria
- Enclosure must be hydrostatically pressure-tested to 1.5 times the derated design pressure (**18.0 bar hydrotest**) prior to live hot-tap or bolt-up installation.
- Dual vent and drain valves (NPS 3/4 Class 800) must remain accessible for leak monitoring.

---
