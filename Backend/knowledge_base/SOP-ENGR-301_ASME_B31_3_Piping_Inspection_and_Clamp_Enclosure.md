**Effective Date**: 15 January 2026

## 1. Wall Thickness Calculation & Remaining Life Formula
Under ASME B31.3 Section 304.1.2, minimum required wall thickness is governed by:

$$t_{m} = \frac{P \cdot D}{2(S \cdot E + P \cdot Y)} + c$$

Where:
- $P$ = Internal design gage pressure (bar / psi)
- $D$ = Outside pipe diameter (mm / in)
- $S$ = Allowable stress value for material at design temperature
- $E$ = Quality factor / longitudinal weld joint factor
- $Y$ = Coefficient for material and temperature range
- $c$ = Mechanical and corrosion allowance

### Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Formula:
$$\text{Remaining Life (Years)} = \frac{t_{\text{actual}} - t_{\text{minimum}}}{\text{Corrosion Rate (mm/year)}}$$

If Remaining Useful Life is determined to be less than **2.0 years**, mandatory management of change (MOC) must be initiated immediately.

## 2. Temporary Engineered Leak Clamps & Mechanical Enclosures
1. In-service bolted clamp enclosures are permitted under ASME PCC-2 Article 204 (Welded and Bolted Repair Enclosures).
2. The enclosure must be designed to withstand the full design pressure without credit for the underlying degraded pipe wall.
3. Continuous NDT inspection frequency must be doubled (every 30 days) while temporary clamp enclosures are in service.
