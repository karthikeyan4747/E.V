# SOP-SAFETY-102: Rotating Equipment Vibration & Toxic Fluid Containment

**Document ID**: SOP-SAFETY-102-2026  
**Category**: Plant Safety & Environmental Protection Policy  
**Standard Authority**: ISO 10816-3 / OSHA 1910.119 Process Safety Management  
**Effective Date**: 01 February 2026  

---

## 1. Machinery Vibration Thresholds (ISO 10816-3 Class III/IV)
- **Normal Zone A/B**: Overall velocity $\le$ 4.5 mm/s RMS (Unrestricted continuous operation)
- **Alert Zone C (Maintenance Threshold)**: 4.5 mm/s to 7.1 mm/s RMS.
  - Mandatory investigation within 48 hours.
  - Daily vibration spectral analysis required.
- **Danger Zone D (Trip / Shutdown Threshold)**: $> 11.2\text{ mm/s RMS}$.
  - Automatic interlock trip or immediate manual shutdown within 15 minutes.

---

## 2. Secondary Containment & Emergency Depressurization
1. Hydrocarbon pumps (CDU bottoms, vacuum residue, and reflux pumps) operating above 200°C must have dual mechanical seals with pressurized barrier fluid (API Plan 53B).
2. Remote operated isolation valves (ROIV) must close within 12 seconds upon DCS fire or hydrocarbon vapor trip signal.
